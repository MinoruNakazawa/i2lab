This page provides English summaries of news and research highlights linked from the English site. Each item links to its original source.

## Developing Humanoid Robots Together with the Workplace {#humanoid-robot}

An introduction to a project that views humanoid robots as part of the workforce and develops their roles together with real workplaces.

[Read the original Japanese article](https://www.kanazawa-it.ac.jp/kitnews/2026/20260507_nanamaru.html)

## KIT and NVIDIA Sign an Academic Collaboration Agreement {#nvidia-collaboration}

Kanazawa Institute of Technology and NVIDIA signed an agreement to advance education and research for the physical-AI era. The collaboration was prompted by NVIDIA's interest in Nakazawa Lab's iREX 2025 exhibition and will expand access to NVIDIA educational programs, AI learning environments, and industry collaboration.

[Read the original Japanese article](https://www.kanazawa-it.ac.jp/kitnews/2026/0217_nvidia.html)

## Nakazawa Lab at iREX 2025 {#irex-2025}

Nakazawa Lab exhibited its research at the 2025 International Robot Exhibition (iREX) at Tokyo Big Sight, held from December 3 to 6, 2025.

[Read the original Japanese article](https://www.kanazawa-it.ac.jp/kitnews/2025/1118_nakazawa.html)
